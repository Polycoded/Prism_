# LumaAir A1 support

Fictional demonstration source. Not Samsung product information.

The LumaAir A1 has a 24-month limited warranty starting on the retail purchase date.

## Maintenance.1

Replace the LumaAir A1 PureAir filter after 180 days of use.

## Setup.1

To reset a LumaAir A1 filter reminder, hold the Filter button for five seconds.
