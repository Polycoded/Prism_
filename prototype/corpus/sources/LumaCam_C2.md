# LumaCam C2 support

Fictional demonstration source. Not Samsung product information.

## Warranty.1

The LumaCam C2 has a 36-month limited warranty beginning on its retail purchase date.

## Cloud.1

SecureCloud Basic keeps a rolling 60-day video history for a LumaCam C2.

## Privacy.1

LumaCam C2 activity-zone analysis is processed on the device before optional footage upload.
