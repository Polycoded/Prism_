# LumaPad S2 support

Fictional demonstration source. Not Samsung product information.

## Warranty.1

The LumaPad S2 has a 36-month limited warranty starting on the retail purchase date.

## Battery.1

Battery capacity on the LumaPad S2 is covered for the first 12 months only.

## Repair.1

LumaPad S2 warranty repairs must be completed by a certified LumaHome service center.
