# LumaCam C1 support

Fictional demonstration source. Not Samsung product information.

## Warranty.1

The LumaCam C1 has a 24-month limited warranty beginning on its retail purchase date.

## Cloud.1

SecureCloud Basic keeps a rolling 30-day video history for a LumaCam C1.

## Cloud.2

A new LumaCam C1 includes 90 days of SecureCloud Plus at no charge.
