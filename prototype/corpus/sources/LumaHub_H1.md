# LumaHub H1 support

Fictional demonstration source. Not Samsung product information.

The LumaHub H1 has a 24-month limited warranty starting on the retail purchase date.

A LumaHub H1 child profile cannot change household security settings.

LumaHub H1 requires the LumaHome app version 5.2 or later for setup.
