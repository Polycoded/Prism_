# CarePlus plan support

Fictional demonstration source. Not Samsung product information.

## Purchase.1

The optional CarePlus plan may be purchased within 30 days of a LumaHome device purchase.

## Coverage.1

CarePlus adds accidental-damage coverage for 24 months after plan activation.

## Cancellation.1

Cancel CarePlus within 14 days of activation for a full plan refund when no claim was made.
