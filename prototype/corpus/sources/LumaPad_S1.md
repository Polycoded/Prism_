# LumaPad S1 support

Fictional demonstration source. Not Samsung product information.

## Warranty.1

The LumaPad S1 has a 24-month limited warranty starting on the retail purchase date.

## Warranty.2

Drops, liquid damage, and cosmetic scratches are excluded from the LumaPad S1 limited warranty.

## Repair.1

A dated purchase receipt and the LumaPad S1 serial number are required to open a warranty repair.
